## Selected digital work

### [KNOQ](https://knoq.au/)

Founder and Venture Architect of a privacy-conscious, location-aware marketplace connecting households and businesses with independent local trades and service professionals. KNOQ is being built as native Android and iOS products supported by a TypeScript API, PostgreSQL and Firebase, with trust, marketplace integrity and controlled location sharing designed in from the start.

### [HarleyShift Advisory](https://harleyshiftadvisory.com.au/)

Founder and Principal Advisor. I designed and built the venture's digital presence around practical executive support for business, AI, technology and transformation decisions. The proposition is deliberately human-led and AI-assisted: technology supports the work; judgement and accountability remain human.

### [BrockMark](https://brockmark.au/)

A complete digital storefront for a young Western Australian maker's custom laser-engraving business. The work combines brand positioning, product presentation, responsive web design, order capture, pricing logic and a straightforward customer journey from discovery to purchase.

### [Frangis After Dark](https://frangisafterdark.au/)

A distinctive editorial and brand-led digital experience, translating a strong creative identity into an immersive, responsive website with considered visual direction and content presentation.

### [Rooh & Root](https://rooh-root.au/)

A design-led digital home for living-art creations. The experience brings together brand story, product care, QR-enabled customer guidance and a consent-controlled pathway for customers to share the continuing story of their piece.

### ClaimsLab

A private proof-of-concept and product-development environment exploring how evidence, workflow and digital experience can improve claims-related work. The source remains private because the project contains commercial and operational material.

## How I build

My projects are developed with a clear bias toward:

- Evidence before assertion
- Privacy, security and trust by design
- Accessible, responsive experiences
- Factual claims and transparent limitations
- Strong governance without unnecessary process
- Practical outcomes over technology theatre

Most commercial and venture repositories are private by design. This profile presents selected outcomes and case-study context without publishing confidential source code, credentials, customer information or unfinished operational material.
